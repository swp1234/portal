Why Week 2 Feels Harder Than Day 1

When novelty fades, your working memory is juggling unfamiliar tools, unwritten norms, and social names all at once—causing legitimate evening exhaustion.

Batch Questions Instead of Suffering in Silence

Collecting 3–4 clear questions for a brief check-in ("I checked the docs and narrowed this down to two options...") signals ownership, not weakness.

Ask for 30-Day Success Criteria Explicitly

Replacing invisible perfectionism with 2 concrete 30-day milestones cuts onboarding anxiety in half overnight.

FAQ

Is it normal to feel completely lost during the first month of a new job?

Yes—learning an organization’s language, tools, and unwritten workflows takes 60 to 90 days for almost every experienced professional.

How many questions is "too many" when onboarding?

Managers love batched, thoughtful questions that show what you already checked; silent guessing causes far bigger mistakes.
