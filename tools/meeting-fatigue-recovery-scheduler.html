    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why are video calls more exhausting than in-person meetings?

    Stanford communication researcher Jeremy Bailenson identified four main mechanisms: intense close-up eye gaze, constant self-view mirror scrutiny, reduced physical mobility, and heightened cognitive load to send and decode nonverbal cues.

    Does a 5-minute break between meetings actually help?

    Yes. Microsoft Human Factors Lab EEG studies found that even brief 5-to-10-minute transitions between back-to-back video meetings prevent cumulative beta-wave stress buildup.
