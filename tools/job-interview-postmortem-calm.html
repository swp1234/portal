    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I obsess over one tiny mistake after a job interview?

    Psychologists call this "post-event processing": under evaluative uncertainty, memory biases toward negative micro-moments while ignoring the majority of answers that went smoothly.

    Does slow recruiter silence always mean rejection?

    Rarely. Hiring panels regularly stall over executive approvals, interviewer travel, or scheduling other finalists—delays of 1–2 weeks are routine even for top candidates.
