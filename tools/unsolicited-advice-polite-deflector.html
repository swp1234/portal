    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does "Don’t JADE" mean in boundary psychology?

    Avoid Justifying, Arguing, Defending, or Explaining your personal decisions to people who did not ask to collaborate—brief polite closure works better.

    What if they keep pushing after my first polite pivot?

    Use a calm broken-record boundary: "I hear you, and I’m not looking for advice on this topic today—let’s switch gears."
