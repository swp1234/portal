    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can you actually pay back sleep debt in a single weekend?

    Sleep research shows that while weekend catch-up sleep helps partially, oversleeping by 2–3+ hours shifts your circadian phase ("social jetlag"). Repaying debt gradually with 30–45 minutes earlier sleep across several nights is gentler on alertness.

    Why does morning daylight help me fall asleep earlier at night?

    Morning bright light signals the brain’s master clock (SCN) to start the 14–16 hour countdown to natural evening melatonin release.
