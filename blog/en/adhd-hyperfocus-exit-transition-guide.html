FAQ

Why do I forget to drink water or sleep while hyperfocusing?

Intense interest narrows attentional bandwidth so internal interoceptive body cues (hunger, thirst, fatigue) temporarily fade into the background.

How can loved ones help me exit hyperfocus smoothly?

Ask for a friendly 5-minute heads-up ("Dinner in 5 minutes—good spot to leave a bookmark?") rather than an abrupt demand to stop immediately.
