    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does gazing at a single visual point for 30 seconds help focus?

    Visual focus and cognitive attention share closely linked neural circuits; holding calm visual focus on one target for 30–60 seconds helps transition the brain out of scattered scanning mode.

    What is attention residue?

    Coined by researcher Sophie Leroy, attention residue is the lingering drop in focus that occurs when part of your mind remains stuck on an unfinished previous task or message.
