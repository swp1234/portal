    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do body-first exercises often calm stress faster than positive thinking?

    Autonomic arousal flows heavily from body to brain via afferent vagal pathways. Extending the exhale and orienting visually directly cues parasympathetic downshifting.

    Is this a medical diagnosis of my nervous system?

    No. This is an educational self-reflection tool designed to help you notice everyday stress cues and choose gentle grounding exercises.
