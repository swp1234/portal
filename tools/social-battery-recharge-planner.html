    Why does social masking drain energy faster than regular conversation?

    Social masking requires constant prefrontal self-monitoring—tracking tone, facial expressions, and unspoken cues—on top of normal sensory processing, which depletes glucose and nervous-system bandwidth far faster than relaxed interaction.

    Does scrolling social media actually recharge a low social battery?

    Usually no. Passive scrolling still delivers rapid social comparison, faces, and auditory stimulation. True decompression comes from low-input quiet (dim lighting, silence or brown noise, walking alone, or light reading).
