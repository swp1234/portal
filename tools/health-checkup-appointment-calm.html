    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does blood pressure rise in a doctor’s office ("white coat effect")?

    Anticipatory alertness and clinical surroundings trigger a temporary sympathetic nervous response. Sitting quietly with uncrossed legs and slow exhales for 3–5 minutes often lowers readings noticeably.

    Does this replace medical guidance?

    No. This is strictly a browser-private anxiety-preparation and question-organizing tool—always follow your licensed clinician’s advice.
