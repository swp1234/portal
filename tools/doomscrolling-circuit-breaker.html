    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does willpower fail to stop late-night doomscrolling?

    By 10 PM, prefrontal executive control is biologically fatigued while variable-ratio short-form feeds exploit low-effort dopamine seeking. Adding physical friction—such as charging your phone across the room or turning on grayscale—interrupts the loop automatically.

    What makes an effective replacement for bedtime scrolling?

    The swap must be nearly zero-friction and mildly engaging without blue-light infinite scroll—such as a paperback book, a familiar low-volume podcast on a sleep timer, or stretching for 5 minutes.
