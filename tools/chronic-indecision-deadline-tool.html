    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a "two-way door" decision?

    A two-way door decision is reversible: if the choice does not work out, you can walk back through the door and adjust with low cost. Spending hours optimizing reversible choices drains energy without improving happiness.

    What is the difference between a maximizer and a satisficer?

    Coined by Nobel laureate Herbert Simon and studied by Barry Schwartz, satisficers choose the first option that meets their clear standards, while maximizers exhaustively compare every option—often experiencing higher stress and post-choice regret.
