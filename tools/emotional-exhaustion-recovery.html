    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between compassion fatigue and general tiredness?

    General tiredness improves after one solid night of sleep. Emotional exhaustion and compassion fatigue come from prolonged emotional caregiving or stress without replenishment, leading to numbness, irritability, and reduced empathy even after sleeping.

    Why is a 48-hour emotional pause more effective than pushing through?

    Lowering emotional output for just 48 hours interrupts chronic cortisol elevation and restores prefrontal emotional regulation before depletion hardens into multi-month burnout.
