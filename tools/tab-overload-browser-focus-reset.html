    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do dozens of open browser tabs make me feel mentally exhausted?

    Every visible tab acts as an open cognitive loop ("unfinished intention"), forcing your brain to repeatedly suppress distractions and draining executive energy.

    How do I close 50 tabs without losing important links?

    Use Bookmark All Tabs (Ctrl+Shift+D on Windows or Cmd+Shift+D on Mac) into a single dated folder—it takes 3 seconds and removes closure anxiety completely.
