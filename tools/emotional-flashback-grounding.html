Frequently Asked Questions

What is an emotional flashback and why does it feel so sudden?

Unlike visual flashbacks, an emotional flashback arrives as a sudden, intense wave of old dread, shame, or helplessness without a clear memory image—making a minor present trigger feel like a total survival crisis.

Why does naming the current year and physical room help interrupt a flashback?

Explicitly orienting to your adult body, today's date, and visible details in the room reactivates the prefrontal cortex and signals the amygdala that the past danger is not happening right now.
