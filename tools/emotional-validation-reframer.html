    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does toxic positivity make anxiety and sadness last longer?

    Research on ironic process theory and emotional suppression shows that forcing yourself not to feel a negative emotion increases physiological arousal and rebound rumination. Validating the feeling first reduces nervous system resistance.

    Does validating a painful emotion mean giving up or wallowing?

    No. Validation simply acknowledges reality without judgment ("This hurts and makes sense"). Once you stop fighting the feeling, constructive next steps become much clearer.
