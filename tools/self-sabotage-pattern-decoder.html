    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do people self-sabotage goals they genuinely care about?

    Psychological research on self-handicapping (Berglas & Jones, 1978) shows procrastination or avoidance often serves as an emotional shield against fearing failure, judgment, or unfamiliar change.

    How do you break a self-sabotage loop without relying on willpower?

    Shrinking the entry step to 3 minutes, separating self-worth from rough drafts, and noticing protective urges with curiosity lowers nervous-system threat far better than self-criticism.
