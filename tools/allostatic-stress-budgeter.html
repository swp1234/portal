    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is allostatic load in stress psychology?

    Introduced by McEwen and Stellar (1993), allostatic load describes the cumulative wear and tear of repeated physiological and psychological stress adaptations over time.

    How does an energy budget help prevent burnout?

    Treating daily attention and emotional stamina as a finite budget makes hidden drains visible early, helping you simplify commitments before chronic exhaustion sets in.
