    Why do people feel exhausted even on days with a light calendar?

    Invisible emotional leaks—such as unresolved open loops, anticipatory worry, people-pleasing, and constant notification switching—consume cognitive and emotional fuel even when no obvious heavy work appears on your calendar.

    How does a 'Stop-Doing' commitment outperform adding another productivity habit?

    When your energy balance is already negative, adding new optimization habits increases friction. Plugging one recurring low-value energy leak frees immediate bandwidth without demanding extra willpower.
