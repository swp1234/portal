    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does having my smartphone on the desk hurt focus even when silent?

    A well-known study by Ward et al. (2017) found that the mere visible presence of a smartphone consumes available cognitive capacity ("brain drain") even when turned face-down or silenced.

    Should I use a 25-minute or 50-minute focus block?

    When mental friction or fatigue is high, a 10- to 25-minute block lowers starting anxiety. When clarity and energy are high, 45–50 minutes lets you stay deeper in flow.
