What Attention Residue Does to Working Memory

Research by Sophie Leroy and Gloria Mark shows that even a 30-second inbox peek leaves cognitive fragments active for 15–23 minutes, slowing complex thinking and increasing errors.

The 1-Tab + Paper Scratchpad Method

When an unrelated thought pops up mid-task, jot 4 words on paper and stay in your current window until the 25-minute timer chimes.

Replace Constant Monitoring With 3 Check-In Windows

Checking Slack/email at 10:00, 13:30, and 16:30 keeps you responsive without shredding every hour into 6-minute slivers.

FAQ

Why does multitasking make me feel so tired?

Every task switch forces your prefrontal cortex to reload rules and context, burning glucose and creating mental fatigue without real progress.

What if my job requires quick replies on Slack or Teams?

Batching checks every 30–45 minutes feels near-instant to colleagues while preserving real uninterrupted blocks for deep work.
