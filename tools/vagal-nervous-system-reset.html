    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does the double-inhale physiological sigh calm the nervous system so quickly?

    A double nasal inhale fully re-inflates tiny air sacs in the lungs (alveoli), maximizing carbon dioxide offload on the extended slow exhale. Long exhalations directly activate the vagus nerve brake to decelerate heart rate.

    Should I use the same reset when I feel frozen or numb as when I feel panicked?

    No. Wired fight-or-flight responds best to slowing down (long exhales, cool water), while numb dorsal shutdown responds better to gentle, non-threatening mobilization (slow orienting, posture shift, soft humming).
