    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between caring deeply and codependency?

    Healthy interdependence allows two people to support each other while keeping separate emotional lanes and honest needs. Codependency blurs those lanes: your calm depends on fixing someone else's mood, and you repeatedly abandon your own rest or limits to avoid disapproval.

    What does 'Detaching With Love' mean in practice?

    Detaching with love means offering warmth and empathy without taking over another adult's choices, consequences, or emotional regulation. You stay compassionate while refusing to rescue at the expense of your own wellbeing.
