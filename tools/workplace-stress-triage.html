    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is acute workplace stress different from clinical burnout?

    Acute stress feels like high urgency and too much pressure, yet energy returns after rest or deadline relief. Burnout feels like chronic emptiness, cynicism, and detachment that persists even after weekends off.

    Why does a 10-minute shutdown ritual stop work stress from following you home?

    Writing down tomorrow’s top 2 tasks closes the brain’s Zeigarnik open-loop tension, while a short walk or breath cycle signals physiological completion of the workday threat response.
