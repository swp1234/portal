    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does a gradual CBT exposure ladder work better than forcing huge social leaps?

    Jumping straight into a 90/100 panic-inducing scenario overwhelms the amygdala and reinforces avoidance. Starting with manageable 25–45 SUDS micro-reps teaches your nervous system through direct experience that awkwardness is survivable.

    What is a SUDS score in social anxiety CBT?

    SUDS stands for Subjective Units of Distress Scale, rated from 0 (completely calm) to 100 (intense distress). Sorting situations by SUDS lets you practice rungs in ascending order.
