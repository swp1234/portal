What the Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR) Actually Is

Neuroendocrinology research shows healthy bodies surge cortisol near dawn to mobilize glucose and alertness. When life stress is elevated, that physical revving feels eerily like panic.

Why Empty-Stomach Coffee + Bedside News Double the Spike

Pouring caffeine and alarming headlines onto peak dawn cortisol multiplies heart rate and jitteriness before you even stand up.

The 10-Minute Physical Discharge Routine

Stand up gently, drink a glass of water, splash cool water on your face, look toward daylight, and eat a small protein bite before caffeine.

FAQ

Why does morning anxiety feel so much worse right after waking?

Cortisol naturally peaks in the early morning while blood sugar is at an overnight low; hydrating, light movement, and food usually settle the wave within 20–30 minutes.

Should I force myself to lie in bed if I wake anxious 30 minutes before my alarm?

No—lying horizontal rehearsing worries intensifies dread; getting up calmly for water and gentle light usually feels far better.
