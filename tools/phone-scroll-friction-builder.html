    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does turning a phone screen to grayscale actually reduce screen time?

    Yes—controlled behavioral studies (Holte & Ferraro, 2020) found switching displays to grayscale reduced daily smartphone usage by 30–40 minutes on average by lowering visual reward salience.

    Why is moving the phone charger out of the bedroom so effective?

    It removes both late-night revenge scrolling and first-thing-in-the-morning cortisol spikes without requiring willpower when tired.
