1. ラグの原因を診断：機器ラグ（FPS低下）vs 通信ラグ（高Ping）

Before changing any settings, identify whether you are suffering from Hardware Lag (Low FPS) or Network Lag (High Ping). Applying graphic tweaks will not resolve a sluggish Wi-Fi connection, and upgrading internet speeds will not fix a strained GPU.

Hardware Lag (FPS Drops)

Symptoms: Choppy character movement, delayed mouse camera panning, stuttering screen when many players or particle effects appear on screen.

Diagnosis Tool: Press Shift + F5 on PC to inspect your frame rate. If it drops below 60, follow Sections 2 through 4.

Network Lag (High Ping)

Symptoms: Other players teleporting, hits or inputs registering several seconds late, "Waiting for Server" notifications, rubber-banding.

Diagnosis Tool: Press Shift + F5 and check Ping. Anything over 120ms indicates connection latency. Follow Section 5.

2. 公式ネイティブFPS解除（最大240 FPS）の設定手順

Roblox officially integrated native frame rate unlocking into the game engine, making high refresh rate gaming safe and seamless.

Step 1

Enable Native Maximum Frame Rate

  1. Launch any Roblox experience on PC.
  2. Press the Esc key on your keyboard to open the in-game menu.
  3. Click on the Settings tab at the top.
  4. Scroll down to the Maximum Frame Rate option.
  5. Switch from Default (60 FPS) to 120 FPS, 144 FPS, or 240 FPS to match your monitor refresh rate.

Note: If your monitor is a standard 60Hz panel, leaving this at 60 or 120 FPS prevents unnecessary GPU power draw and thermal throttling.

3. BloxstrapとFastFlagsの最新事情

Many legacy guides recommend pasting lengthy lists of FastFlags (JSON strings) into custom launchers like Bloxstrap to claim "1000+ FPS." Here is the reality in late 2026:

  • Engine Allowlist: Roblox deployed engine-level protections that automatically filter and ignore unrecognized FastFlags (including legacy scheduler overrides like DFIntTaskSchedulerTargetFps).
  • Visual Artifacts & Crashes: Forcing experimental renderer flags can lead to missing textures, invisible player models, or sudden client crashes during live rounds.
  • Safe Bloxstrap Usage: While Bloxstrap is still a fantastic open-source wrapper for custom fonts, server location displays, and launcher perks, do not rely on outdated FastFlag scripts for performance. Use native in-game settings and Windows GPU scheduling instead.

4. Windows 11/10 高性能GPU割り当て設定

Windows frequently misassigns the Roblox client to integrated CPU graphics instead of your dedicated Nvidia GeForce or AMD Radeon GPU. Correcting this takes 60 seconds and yields immediate frame rate improvements.

Step 2

Force Dedicated High-Performance GPU

  1. Press Windows Key + I to open Windows Settings.
  2. Navigate to System > Display > Graphics.
  3. Under "Custom options for apps," click Browse and locate your Roblox client executable (RobloxPlayerBeta.exe in %localappdata%\Roblox\Versions).
  4. Click Options, select High Performance (Dedicated GPU), and click Save.
Step 3

Enable Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling (HAGS)

In the Graphics settings window, click on Change default graphics settings. Toggle Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling to On. This offloads frame scheduling tasks from your CPU directly to GPU memory, eliminating micro-stutters during heavy combat.

5. 破損したキャッシュファイルのクリーンアップ

Over months of playing, fragmented meshes, audio clips, and textures accumulate locally, causing slow asset loading and stuttering.

Step 4

Safe Cache Flush (Zero Data Loss)

  1. Completely close Roblox and the background launcher.
  2. Press Windows Key + R, type %temp%, hit Enter, and delete temporary files.
  3. Press Windows Key + R, type %localappdata%\Roblox, hit Enter, and delete the contents of the Downloads folder. Fresh copies will download automatically.

7. ネットワークラグ＆高Pingの改善策

  • Flush DNS Cache: Open Command Prompt (CMD) as Administrator and type ipconfig /flushdns.
  • Prioritize 5GHz or Ethernet: Switching to 5GHz Wi-Fi or a wired Ethernet cable immediately stabilizes ping spikes.
  • Select Closer Regional Servers: Games like Rivals or Blade Ball feature custom server browsers in their lobbies. Always select servers geographically closer to you.

8. スマホ（iPhone・Android）の発熱＆カクつき防止

  • Remove Bulky Phone Cases: Trapped heat causes your phone CPU to thermally throttle down to 20-30 FPS.
  • Turn Off Low Power Mode: Battery Saver intentionally caps CPU clock speeds. Keep your phone charged above 30%.
  • Disable Background App Refresh: Prevent social media and video apps from consuming RAM while gaming.
  • Clear App Cache (Android): Go to Settings > Apps > Roblox > Storage > Clear Cache weekly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get banned for uncapping FPS in Roblox?

No. Roblox officially added native frame rate controls directly into the client settings menu. Selecting 120, 144, or 240 FPS inside Roblox is 100% endorsed by Roblox Corporation and will never result in an account suspension.

Why is Roblox lagging even on a high-end PC?

Roblox experiences are predominantly single-core CPU limited. Unoptimized developer scripts, heavy physics simulation, or corrupt local caches can drop frames even on high-end hardware. Setting graphics quality to 5-7 and clearing cache provides the smoothest baseline.

Does clearing Roblox cache delete my account or Robux?

No. Clearing temporary files only deletes downloaded 3D assets, textures, and sound files. Your username, password, inventory, Robux balance, and game data are safely preserved in the cloud.

How do you uncap FPS on Roblox mobile?

Roblox mobile clients are locked to 60 FPS to prevent battery drain and thermal overheating. Modded APKs or unauthorized injection tools violate the Terms of Service. Stick to standard in-game graphics level 2 or 3 for maximum stability.
