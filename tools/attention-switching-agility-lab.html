    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is attention residue when switching tasks?

    Coined by Dr. Sophie Leroy, attention residue describes the portion of your attention that stays stuck on an unfinished previous task, lowering focus quality on the next one.

    Why does writing a 1-line parking note help?

    Capturing the exact next step before leaving a task signals closure to your brain, dramatically reducing intrusive thoughts about the paused item.
