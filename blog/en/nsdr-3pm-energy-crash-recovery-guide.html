Why 2–4 PM Feels So Heavy Biologically

Core body temperature dips slightly ~7–8 hours after waking, creating a natural alertness trough that refined-carb lunches intensify sharply.

The 10-Minute Post-Lunch Walk Glucose Trick

Light walking right after eating lets leg muscles absorb glucose smoothly, preventing the sharp spike-and-crash loop behind food coma.

10-Minute NSDR (Non-Sleep Deep Rest) Instead of Scrolling

Close your eyes, silence notifications, and slow your exhale for 10 minutes—giving your visual cortex real recovery without deep-nap grogginess.

FAQ

Does 10 minutes of eyes-closed NSDR work even if I don’t fall asleep?

Yes! That is the main advantage of NSDR—your nervous system downshifts and restores focus without entering deep sleep, so you wake up crisp with zero grogginess.

Should I schedule easy or hard tasks at 3 PM?

Reserve morning peak hours for demanding analytical work and slot routine replies, admin, or collaborative check-ins into the 2:30–4:00 PM trough.
