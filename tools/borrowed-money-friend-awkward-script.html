    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does referencing "monthly budget cleanup" make asking for money back easier?

    It frames the reminder around your own neutral routine rather than accusing the friend of avoidance, letting them save face and pay immediately.

    What if they ignore my second polite reminder?

    Switch to a clear, direct check-in with an easy date: "Hey, just checking in on the $X from last month—does Friday work to settle it, or would splitting it in two help?"
