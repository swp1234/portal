Spending 8 years building a career feels like proof you must stay—yet those 8 years are already spent either way. The only real question is where the next 5 years compound better.

FAQ

How do I know if I need a career change or just a new company?

If you still enjoy the core craft when rested but dread leadership, pay, or culture, try changing teams first; if the daily tasks themselves drain you even after vacation, explore a pivot.

Do I have to quit my job to test a new career?

No—informational interviews, small freelance/volunteer projects, and weekend prototypes let you validate fit with zero income disruption.
