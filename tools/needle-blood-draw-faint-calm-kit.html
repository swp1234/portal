    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does standard deep relaxation sometimes make needle fainting worse?

    Vasovagal needle fainting is caused by a sudden dip in blood pressure and heart rate; Öst’s Applied Tension gently raises blood pressure using leg/body muscles (while keeping the draw arm relaxed) to prevent lightheadedness.

    Can I drink plain water before a fasting blood test?

    For most standard fasting blood panels, plain water is encouraged because hydration makes veins easier to find and helps prevent lightheadedness—always confirm with your lab’s specific instructions.
