    What are cognitive distortions in CBT?

    Cognitive distortions are automatic, biased thinking habits—such as catastrophizing, all-or-nothing thinking, or mind reading—that intensify stress and skew interpretation. Labeling them objectively interrupts the spiral.

    How does Socratic reframing work?

    Socratic questioning tests an automatic thought against observable facts, alternative explanations, and self-compassion so your brain can shift from threat reactivity to realistic problem solving.
