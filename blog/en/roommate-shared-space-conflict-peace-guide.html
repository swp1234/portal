Why "Our House System" Works Better Than "You Never Clean"

Blaming character puts people instantly on defense; designing a concrete default ("trash goes out when full, dishes within 12h") invites teamwork.

The 3 Default Agreements Every Shared Home Needs

Agree explicitly on: 1) Kitchen reset window, 2) Weeknight quiet hours, and 3) 24-hour heads-up for overnight guests.

FAQ

How do I bring up dirty dishes without sounding controlling?

Use neutral system language: "I love keeping the kitchen easy to cook in—can we agree on a same-day dish reset for both of us?"

Should I leave a sticky note or talk in person?

A brief, friendly daytime chat or warm text proposing a 10-min house check-in works far better than blunt sticky notes.
