FAQ

What is the Cognitive Shuffle for falling asleep faster?

It is a gentle imagery exercise where you pick a neutral word and visualize unrelated harmless objects starting with each letter, interrupting logical worry so natural sleep takes over.

Why shouldn’t I force myself to "think of nothing" in bed?

Trying hard to suppress thoughts creates mental rebound tension; giving your mind effortless, random neutral images relaxes vigilance naturally.
