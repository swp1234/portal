    Frequently Asked Questions

    Will dentists or doctors be annoyed if I say I am anxious?

    Not at all—clinicians see anxious patients every day and appreciate knowing upfront so they can pace gently and explain each step.

    What helps most in the waiting room right before being called?

    Slow exhales longer than inhales (4s in, 6s out), relaxing your jaw and hands, and grounding your feet flat on the floor.
