    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do crowded subway cars trigger sudden dizziness or heat flashes?

    Heavy coats inside warm train cars raise skin temperature rapidly while standing tightly restricts airflow—unzipping before boarding and sipping cool water counters the physical trigger immediately.

    Which train car is usually calmest for anxious commuters?

    The very front or rear cars (furthest from central transfer escalators) are consistently less packed and let you stand comfortably near a wall or door.
