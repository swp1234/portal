## What Makes DopaBrain Wellness Lab Different?

1. **Zero Data Leaves Your Browser** — Every thought reframing entry, trigger log, focus audit, and sleep calculation runs 100% locally in client-side JavaScript (`localStorage` only, zero server accounts or database storage).
2. **140 Specialized Interactive Tools Across 5 Core Pillars** — Anxiety & Nervous System, Burnout & Work Boundaries, Relationships & Attachment, ADHD & Deep Focus, and Sleep & Circadian Rhythm.
3. **Instant 1080×1920 Story Cards & 60s Calm Pacer** — Save personal takeaways locally as crisp visual cards or run a built-in 60-second slow-exhale physiological sigh pacer right on the page.
4. **12-Language Native Experience** — Seamless `?lang=` URL preservation across English, Korean, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish, and Russian.
5. **Non-Clinical, Evidence-Grounded Transparency** — Clear non-diagnostic disclaimers, peer-reviewed psychology citations, and zero dark patterns or paywalled results.
