FAQ

Why do I feel burned out every December even when taking time off?

Low winter daylight, stacked social/gift logistics, and unclosed work threads keep stress elevated unless you consciously close or defer open loops.

How do I politely push a non-urgent work request to January?

Offer a specific date: "To keep quality high on our top deliverables before break, let’s schedule this for Jan 8—does that work?"
