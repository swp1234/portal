    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can normal commercial turbulence flip or drop a modern airliner?

    No—modern passenger aircraft are engineered and tested to withstand loads far beyond severe atmospheric turbulence. Seatbelts are worn to protect passengers from bumping inside the cabin.

    Why do engine sounds get quieter shortly after takeoff?

    Pilots routinely reduce engines from full takeoff thrust to climb thrust around 1,000–1,500 feet for noise abatement and efficiency—it is completely normal.
