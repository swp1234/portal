    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does a 10-minute Sunday review reduce Monday anxiety so noticeably?

    Capturing unorganized open tasks externally stops the Zeigarnik Effect—where unfinished loops keep pinging working memory—so you sleep better Sunday night and start Monday with clarity.

    How many weekly goals should I pick during a mental clarity review?

    Limiting weekly priorities to 3 concrete wins prevents overwhelm and dramatically raises completion rates compared to 15-item wish lists.
