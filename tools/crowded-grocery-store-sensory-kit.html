    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do supermarket fluorescent lights make me feel spaced out or dizzy?

    Bright high-flicker overhead lighting combined with visual shelf clutter overstimulates visual-vestibular processing—wearing a cap or light-tinted lenses often helps immediately.

    Is it okay to wear headphones while grocery shopping?

    Yes—thousands of HSP, neurodivergent, and busy shoppers wear earbuds in stores to cut noise fatigue (just pause one side at checkout).
