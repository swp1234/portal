    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does perfectionism often look like procrastination in an inbox?

    When every small message feels like a test of competence, your brain flags routine replies as high-threat, causing avoidance and guilt spirals (Hewitt & Flett).

    What is the "B+ Done" triage rule?

    Deciding upfront that ~80% of routine communication and admin only needs to be clear, kind, and accurate—saving A+ perfection for the 1–2 flagship tasks that truly benefit from it.
