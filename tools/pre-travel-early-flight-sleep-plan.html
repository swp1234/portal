    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why shouldn’t I go to bed at 8 PM before a 4 AM flight?

    Your circadian alerting signal peaks in the early evening ("wake maintenance zone"), so forcing bed 3 hours early usually causes frustrating tossing and turning rather than extra sleep.

    How do I stop waking up every hour checking my alarm?

    Set two independent alarms (phone on charger + backup watch/clock 5 min later), confirm volume once, and turn the clock face away so your brain stops guarding the time.
