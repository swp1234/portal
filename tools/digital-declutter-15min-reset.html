    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do 40 open browser tabs make me feel anxious even when I ignore them?

    Each open tab acts as an unfinished visual task cue, subtly draining cognitive bandwidth and reminding your brain of unread obligations.

    How do I close tabs without worrying I will lose something important?

    Use "Bookmark All Tabs" into a dated folder—closing them clears visual clutter immediately while preserving 100% of the links.
