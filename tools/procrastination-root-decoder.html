    Why is procrastination an emotion-regulation problem, not laziness?

    Research by Tim Pychyl and Fuschia Sirois shows chronic procrastination is driven by short-term mood repair—avoiding anxiety, self-doubt, boredom, or ambiguity attached to a task—rather than poor time math.

    How does the 5-minute rule overcome task paralysis?

    Committing to just 5 minutes lowers perceived threat enough for the brain to bypass avoidance. Once started, momentum (the Zeigarnik effect) makes continuing dramatically easier.
