    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does deciding what to eat for dinner feel disproportionately exhausting?

    By 6 PM, you have already made hundreds of work and micro-decisions. Household cognitive load research (Daminger, 2019) shows anticipating needs, identifying options, and deciding drains executive function even before cooking starts.

    Won’t repeating weekday meal themes get boring?

    Themes (like "taco/bowl night" or "pasta night") remove 90% of blank-page friction while still letting you swap proteins, sauces, or veggies freely.
