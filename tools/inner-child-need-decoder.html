    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does 'self-reparenting' mean in schema and inner-child work?

    Self-reparenting means meeting your own present-day emotional needs—such as steady reassurance, clear boundaries, rest, and warmth—with the consistent care you wished a calm adult had offered earlier in life.
