    What is the Window of Tolerance?

    Coined by psychiatrist Dr. Dan Siegel, the Window of Tolerance is the optimal zone of nervous system arousal where you can process stress, think clearly, and stay socially connected without flipping into panic (hyperarousal) or shutdown (hypoarousal).

    Why do hyperarousal and hypoarousal need different regulation techniques?

    Hyperarousal (fight-or-flight) involves excess sympathetic activation and benefits from down-regulation such as extended exhales and cool sensory grounding. Hypoarousal (freeze or numbness) involves dorsal shutdown and benefits from gentle mobilization, visual orienting, and warm micro-movements rather than passive stillness.
