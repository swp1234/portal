FAQ

How do I close dozens of tabs quickly without losing anything important?

Use Bookmark All Tabs (Ctrl+Shift+D on Windows or Cmd+Shift+D on Mac) to store the entire window in one dated folder in 3 seconds, then open a clean 3-tab window.

How many browser tabs should I keep open for deep work?

Limiting your active window to 1–3 task-specific tabs dramatically lowers context switching and visual fatigue.
