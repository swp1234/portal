    What is executive function friction (task paralysis)?

    Executive function friction occurs when the brain's planning, initiation, or working-memory circuits become overloaded or under-stimulated, making even simple tasks feel physically impossible to start despite caring deeply about completing them.

    Why does body doubling help with ADHD and focus blocks?

    Body doubling uses gentle external structure and periodic pacing cues as a psychological anchor, reducing decision fatigue and lowering the activation energy needed to stay on one bounded task.
