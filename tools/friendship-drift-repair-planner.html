    Frequently Asked Questions

    Will an old friend think it is weird if I text after a year of silence?

    Empirical studies (Liu et al., 2022) show the exact opposite: recipients feel significantly more appreciated and happier than senders predict—especially when the gap has been long.

    What is the easiest opener to break months of silence?

    Reference a specific small thing that reminded you of them ("Walked past X and thought of you—hope you’re doing well, zero pressure to rush a reply!") rather than over-apologizing.
