    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does the 20-20-20 rule really reduce digital eye strain?

    Optometry guidance (American Optometric Association) recommends looking at an object 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes to relax sustained near-focus ciliary tension.

    Why does panoramic vision also calm stress?

    Shifting from narrow foveal focus to wider panoramic peripheral awareness engages parasympathetic calming pathways and eases visual tunnel tension.
