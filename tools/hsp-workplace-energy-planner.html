    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do highly sensitive people feel more drained in open offices?

    Sensory processing sensitivity (Aron & Aron, 1997) involves deeper cognitive and sensory processing of subtle environmental cues, meaning constant background noise and emotional tension consume more bandwidth.

    Can I protect my sensory energy without disclosing "HSP" at work?

    Yes. Framing needs around work quality—such as "heads-down focus time," "written recaps," and "short buffers between meetings"—is standard professional practice.
