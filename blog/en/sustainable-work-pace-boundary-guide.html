FAQ

Does holding firm work hours hurt career growth?

Consistent, rested execution during agreed hours regularly beats exhausted overtime in decision quality, creativity, and long-term sustainability.

How do I decline extra rush work without sounding difficult?

Acknowledge the request warmly and offer a clear priority trade-off so your manager chooses what moves.
