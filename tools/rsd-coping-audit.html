    What is rejection sensitivity (RSD)?

    Rejection sensitivity describes sharp emotional pain, shame, or hypervigilance triggered by perceived criticism or social ambiguity. Recognizing the pattern helps you pause before reacting.

    Why does the 90-second pause help?

    Initial sympathetic adrenaline spikes crest and subside within roughly 90 seconds if we do not re-trigger them with rumination or defensive messages.
