    What is the difference between productive problem-solving and rumination?

    Problem-solving leads to a concrete, time-bounded action you can take within 24 hours. Rumination replays the same unanswerable question or past regret without producing a new step, draining emotional energy while creating an illusion of control.

    How does the 90-second Cognitive Shuffle calm racing thoughts at night?

    Developed by cognitive scientist Dr. Luc Beaudoin, the Cognitive Shuffle redirects working memory toward random, emotionally neutral visual images (like a wooden sailboat or warm teacup), signaling to the brain that no urgent analytical threat requires staying awake.
