    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why can’t I start a task even when I know it only takes 10 minutes?

    Task paralysis often stems from high activation energy, unclear micro-steps, or emotional friction rather than laziness. Separating setup from execution lowers that wall.

    Does body-doubling or background music really help task initiation?

    For many people with executive friction, mild external structure (a co-working video, timer, or instrumental track) provides enough baseline engagement to cross the starting line.
