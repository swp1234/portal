Why Overhead White LEDs Delay Melatonin

Bright overhead lighting suppresses pineal melatonin release even after you put your phone down; switching to one low warm lamp 45 minutes prior changes sleep onset fast.

The Warm-Shower Core Temperature Drop Effect

Meta-analysis (Haghayegh et al., 2019) showed a warm bath or shower 1–2 hours before bed accelerates sleep onset by drawing blood to the skin and cooling the body’s core afterward.

The 15-15-15 Repeating Cue Trains Sleepiness

Doing the same gentle 3-step glide nightly conditions your nervous system to yawn right on cue.

FAQ

Why does a warm shower help me fall asleep if cool rooms are better for sleep?

Warm water dilates surface blood vessels so heat radiates out rapidly afterward, triggering the core body temperature drop that signals sleep onset.

What if I only have 20 minutes to wind down on busy nights?

Shrink it to 5-5-10: dim lights immediately, wash face/hands warm, and read 10 minutes of light fiction away from work chats.
