    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does morning sunlight improve both daytime energy and nighttime sleep?

    Natural morning light hitting retinal melanopsin receptors triggers a healthy cortisol awakening response and sets an internal 14–16 hour countdown timer for evening melatonin release.

    Does an effective morning routine need to take 2 hours?

    No. A consistent 10-to-20 minute launch sequence (hydration, natural light, delayed scrolling, and one clear priority) outperforms elaborate routines that collapse after three days.
