    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do tiny decisions like meals or inbox replies cause evening brain fog?

    Every micro-choice uses the same prefrontal executive budget as high-stakes problem solving. By late afternoon, hundreds of low-value choices trigger decision fatigue—leading to avoidance, impulsivity, or blank-stare exhaustion.

    How do 'Default Rules' eliminate decision fatigue?

    Making one upstream policy decision (such as rotating 3 weekday breakfasts or batching messages twice a day) removes dozens of repetitive micro-decisions every single week.
