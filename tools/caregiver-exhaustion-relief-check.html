    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do caregivers feel guilty even when completely exhausted?

    Deep love, cultural duty norms, and seeing a loved one’s needs up close can trick caregivers into believing rest equals abandonment. In reality, planned respite reduces errors, irritability, and health decline.

    How do I get family members to actually help?

    Open-ended requests often stall because people do not know what is needed. Offering two concrete choices ("Can you pick up prescriptions Tuesday OR sit with Mom Saturday 2–4 PM?") dramatically increases follow-through.
