    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does writing a 5-minute bedtime to-do list help you fall asleep faster?

    A Baylor University sleep lab study (Scullin et al., 2018) showed participants who wrote specific upcoming tasks before bed fell asleep significantly faster than those who wrote about completed tasks, because offloading reduces cognitive arousal.

    What is cognitive shuffling for racing bedtime thoughts?

    Developed by cognitive scientist Dr. Luc Beaudoin, picturing unrelated neutral images mimics the fragmented mental imagery of sleep onset and gently interrupts problem-solving worry loops.
