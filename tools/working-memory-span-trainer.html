    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many items can human working memory hold comfortably at once?

    Cognitive psychology research (Cowan, 2001) indicates that active working memory comfortably holds about 4 meaningful chunks at a time; exceeding that creates attention residue and mental fatigue.

    Does closing open tabs and offloading tasks actually improve focus?

    Yes. Externalizing open loops onto paper or a list reduces Zeigarnik-effect background rehearsal, freeing mental bandwidth for the task directly in front of you.
