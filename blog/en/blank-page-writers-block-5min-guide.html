Why Editing Sentence 1 Kills Momentum

Trying to sound publish-ready on line one triggers error-monitoring circuits that shut down associative fluency. You can always polish a messy draft—never a blank page.

The Journalism "[TK]" Trick That Stops Research Rabbit Holes

Whenever a date or stat is missing, type "[TK]" and keep typing so you never lose flow to 12 browser tabs.

Talk Like Texting a Friend for Paragraph One

Begin with "Basically what I want to say is..."—you can delete that scaffold in editing once the real idea is on screen.

FAQ

Why does banning Backspace for 5 minutes work so well?

It physically enforces separation between creative generation and critical editing, letting momentum build before perfectionism intervenes.

Should I always write the introduction last?

Yes—writing the easiest middle section first clarifies your real thesis, making the introduction effortless afterward.
