    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between empathy distress and compassion?

    Social neuroscience (Singer & Klimecki, 2014) shows empathetic distress mirrors another’s pain and leads to burnout, whereas compassion pairs warm concern with healthy emotional boundaries and resilience.

    Why does saying "I have 20 minutes" feel kinder than listening until exhausted?

    Clear time containers let you stay genuinely present during the conversation instead of pulling away or feeling resentful afterward.
