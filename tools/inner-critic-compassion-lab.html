    Why is self-compassion more effective than harsh self-criticism?

    Research by Dr. Kristin Neff shows harsh self-criticism activates the brain's threat-defense system (cortisol and avoidance), whereas mindful self-compassion restores safety and actually increases accountability and follow-through.

    What are the 3 pillars of self-compassion?

    The 3 pillars are: (1) Mindfulness — naming pain clearly without over-identifying with it; (2) Common Humanity — remembering imperfection is a shared human experience; and (3) Self-Kindness — speaking to yourself as you would to a trusted friend.
