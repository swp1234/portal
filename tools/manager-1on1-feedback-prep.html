    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I stop panicking when my manager sends a "Quick chat?" message?

    Remember that 90% of quick pings are routine logistics; reply warmly asking "Sure—anything I should pull up beforehand?" to turn mystery into clarity.

    What should I say if I feel surprised by critical feedback?

    You never have to debate on the spot: thank them for raising it, write down the exact example, and ask for a day to reflect and follow up with a plan.
