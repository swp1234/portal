    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between fragile self-esteem and grounded self-worth?

    Self-esteem often fluctuates with external comparisons and achievements ("I am valuable when I win"). Grounded self-worth and self-compassion remain steady through mistakes, rest, and ordinary human imperfection.

    How does shadow integration support healthier self-worth?

    In Jungian psychology, disowning tiring or "unacceptable" traits (anger, neediness, fatigue) fuels hidden shame. Acknowledging those human parts with calm boundaries reduces perfectionism and emotional exhaustion.
