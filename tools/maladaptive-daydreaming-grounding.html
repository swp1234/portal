    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does music often trigger vivid daydreaming loops?

    Research on maladaptive daydreaming (Somer et al., 2016) notes that rhythmic music and kinesthetic movement (like pacing) strongly cue immersive fantasy narratives.

    Is trying to force myself never to daydream effective?

    Rigid suppression often creates shame and rebound episodes. Gently shifting environmental cues and addressing underlying stress, boredom, or loneliness works much better.
