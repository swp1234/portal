Why is JADE (Justify, Argue, Defend, Explain) counterproductive when setting boundaries?

Over-explaining invites negotiation and signals self-doubt. Clear, brief, respectful statements preserve both your energy and relationship clarity.

What is the broken-record boundary technique?

When someone pushes back repeatedly, the broken-record technique calmly repeats your core boundary using nearly identical wording without escalating emotion.
