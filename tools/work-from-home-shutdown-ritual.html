    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is a physical shutdown ritual so effective when working from home?

    When your desk sits inside your home, environmental cues blur together. A consistent physical sequence (writing tomorrow’s list, closing the laptop out of sight, changing clothes/lighting) trains your brain to switch off work vigilance.

    What is the Zeigarnik effect in remote-work stress?

    Psychologist Bluma Zeigarnik observed that unfinished tasks stay louder in memory than finished ones; writing down a concrete plan for tomorrow satisfies the brain’s tracking loop so it lets go for the evening (Masicampo & Baumeister, 2011).
