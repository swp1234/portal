    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does a delayed text trigger intense physical abandonment panic?

    For brains wired by inconsistent early care or past betrayals, ambiguity registers in the amygdala as immediate social threat—releasing cortisol and adrenaline until certainty returns.

    How does a 20-minute self-soothing pause strengthen relationships?

    Waiting 20 minutes allows the acute survival surge to subside so you communicate from calm self-respect rather than protest texting or cold withdrawal.
