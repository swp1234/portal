    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I feel terrible guilt even when my boundary is completely reasonable?

    If you grew up keeping the peace by accommodating everyone, your nervous system mislabels normal self-protection as "doing something wrong" until it gets used to healthy limits.

    What if family keeps pushing after I say no politely?

    Use the calm broken-record technique: acknowledge their feeling once ("I hear you’re disappointed") and repeat the exact same brief limit without adding new excuses.
