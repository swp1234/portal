    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is gaslighting different from a normal disagreement or miscommunication?

    Normal disagreements involve two people holding different interpretations while respecting each other's memory and feelings. Gaslighting is a repeated pattern that erodes your trust in documented facts, reverses blame when you raise concerns (DARVO), and leaves you chronically apologizing for bringing up valid boundaries.

    Why does writing down one objective fact help break manipulation fog?

    Emotional manipulation thrives on circular arguments and vague accusations. Recording one verifiable, timestamped observation locally restores cognitive grounding and stops you from getting pulled into endless justification loops.
