    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does avoiding bank accounts make financial anxiety worse?

    When exact figures stay unknown, the amygdala fills in catastrophic worst-case estimates—a pattern behavioral economists call the "ostrich effect." Seeing real numbers in a brief daylight window lowers uncertainty rapidly.
