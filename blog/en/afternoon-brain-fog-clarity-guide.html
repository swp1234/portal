FAQ

Why does stale office air cause sudden brain fog?

Poor ventilation allows exhaled CO₂ to climb above 1,000–1,400 ppm, which controlled Harvard studies linked directly to slower strategic thinking and fatigue.

How quickly does water help mild dehydration fog?

Rehydrating with 350–500ml water plus a brief 5-minute walk typically improves alertness and focus within 15–20 minutes.
