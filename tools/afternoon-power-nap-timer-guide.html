    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I feel worse after a 45-minute nap than after a 20-minute nap?

    Around minute 30 you enter deep slow-wave sleep; waking out of deep sleep causes heavy "sleep inertia" grogginess, whereas a 15–20 minute nap stays in light refreshing Stage-2 sleep.

    Does a 20-minute nap still help if I do not actually fall fully asleep?

    Yes! Quiet eyes-closed rest (Non-Sleep Deep Rest / NSDR) still lowers visual-cortex load and restores attention even if you stay lightly aware.
