    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does functional freeze feel like laziness even though you are exhausted?

    In Polyvagal Theory, dorsal-vagal shutdown slows metabolic and emotional output to protect an overwhelmed system—often right underneath high-functioning outer habits, creating heavy inertia without true rest.

    Why start with tiny physical movements instead of a motivational pep talk?

    Shutdown lives in subcortical survival circuitry below verbal logic. Gentle bottom-up cues (warmth, eye orienting, slow joint motion) signal safety far faster than harsh self-criticism.
