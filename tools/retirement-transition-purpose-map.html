    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do big positive transitions like retirement or graduation sometimes feel strangely empty?

    Author William Bridges described transitions as having three phases: an Ending, a disorienting Neutral Zone, and a New Beginning. Losing an external daily timetable temporarily creates a vacuum until you build self-chosen anchors.

    What predicts happiness most strongly in later life chapters?

    The 85-year Harvard Study of Adult Development consistently found that warm, regular social relationships—paired with a sense of daily purpose and movement—outweigh wealth or prestige for lifelong well-being.
