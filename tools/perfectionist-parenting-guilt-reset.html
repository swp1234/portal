    Frequently Asked Questions

    What did Donald Winnicott mean by the "good-enough parent"?

    Pediatrician and psychoanalyst D. W. Winnicott observed that children do not need flawless parents; mild everyday frustrations paired with reliable love and repair actually help kids build healthy emotional resilience.

    Does apologizing to my child after losing my temper hurt my authority?

    Not at all. Modeling a calm, honest apology teaches children accountability, emotional regulation, and trust that relationships can always be repaired.
