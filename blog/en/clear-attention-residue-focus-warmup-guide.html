What Attention Residue Does Inside Working Memory

When you leave Task A unfinished to jump into Task B, part of your executive attention stays stuck rehearsing Task A—shrinking available RAM for complex thinking.

The "Brain Drain" Effect of a Smartphone on Your Desk

University of Texas experiments (Ward et al., 2017) proved merely keeping your smartphone visible on your desk lowers working-memory capacity—even when muted and face down.

The 120-Second Pre-Session Cognitive Warmup

Write any nagging errand on a sticky note, move your phone into another room or drawer, go full-screen on one window, and gaze calmly at one spot for 30 seconds before typing line 1.

FAQ

How long does attention residue last after switching tasks?

Organizational research shows significant cognitive drag persists for 15 to 25 minutes after an unclosed interruption unless you intentionally close or park the prior loop.

Does putting my phone face down on the desk stop distraction?

Studies show putting the phone completely out of sight (in a bag, drawer, or another room) restores noticeably more working memory than simply turning it face down.
