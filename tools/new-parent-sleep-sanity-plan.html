    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does one 4-hour stretch of sleep help new parents so much?

    Consolidating at least 4 unbroken hours lets the brain complete multiple full slow-wave and REM cycles—protecting emotional regulation, memory, and safety far better than fragmented 60-minute stretches.

    Is this medical or postpartum clinical advice?

    No. This is an educational self-reflection and pacing planner that stays 100% private in your browser. If you experience persistent postpartum depression or anxiety symptoms, please contact your healthcare provider.
