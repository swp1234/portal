    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does switching careers mean throwing away my previous years of experience?

    Rarely. Most successful career pivots combine 60–80% existing transferable skills (communication, analysis, domain context) with 20–40% new craft.

    How can I test a new career without quitting my current job first?

    Conduct 2–3 brief 15-minute informational interviews, build one weekend mini-project, or volunteer on a cross-functional task inside your current company.
