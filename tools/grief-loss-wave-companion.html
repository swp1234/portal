    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why doesn’t grief follow the 5 stages in order?

    Modern bereavement research—especially Stroebe and Schut’s Dual-Process Model—shows grief is non-linear. People naturally oscillate between confronting loss and taking restorative breaks in daily life.

    Is this grief companion a substitute for grief counseling or crisis support?

    No. This is a free, private self-reflection aid for daily pacing. If grief feels overwhelming or unsafe, please reach out to a licensed grief counselor or local crisis helpline.
