    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does trying to write well on the first try cause creative block?

    Creating (divergent thinking) and editing (convergent judgment) rely on competing neural networks. Trying to polish line one immediately stalls associative flow.

    What is the "Hemingway Bridge" for creative consistency?

    Stopping a session while you still clearly see the next sentence or step—jotting a 1-line note—so you never face a cold blank page the next morning.
