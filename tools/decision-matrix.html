FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Decision Matrix Science & FAQ

What is a Weighted Decision Matrix and how does it resolve decision fatigue?

A Weighted Decision Matrix (or Pugh Matrix) is an analytical framework that scores alternative options against weighted criteria. By separating criteria importance (1–5) from performance scores (1–10), it breaks emotional overwhelm into objective calculations, eliminating cognitive bias and analysis paralysis.

How should I assign weights to criteria?

Assign weights based on true life impact: 1 for minor preferences or short-term conveniences, 3 for standard considerations, and 5 for core non-negotiable values like health, psychological wellbeing, or financial stability.

What should I do if the final scores are very close?

When two options score within a narrow margin (under 8%), both paths are objectively viable. Re-examine your highest-weighted criteria (Weight 5) to see which option wins on non-negotiable fundamentals, or consult your somatic stress response.

Is my decision data stored on any server?

No. All calculations run strictly client-side inside your browser. No option names, notes, weights, or scores are logged, saved to external databases, or shared with third parties.
