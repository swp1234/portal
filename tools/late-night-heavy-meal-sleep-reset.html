    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does resting on your left side feel more comfortable after a late meal?

    Because of stomach anatomy, lying on the left side keeps the stomach curvature lower than the esophageal junction, reducing upward discomfort.

    Is this medical treatment for GERD or chronic reflux?

    No—this is a general everyday sleep-comfort habit planner; please see a doctor if you experience frequent or severe heartburn.
