    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long before bed should I stop drinking caffeine?

    A 2023 systematic review in Sleep Medicine Reviews recommends cutting standard coffee (~100mg) at least 8.8 hours before bedtime—and high-dose drinks (200mg+) up to 12–13 hours before bed—to protect deep sleep.

    Why do I feel tired even when I fall asleep easily after evening coffee?

    Caffeine can still suppress deep slow-wave brain activity by 15–20% even in people who fall asleep quickly, causing groggy mornings and a cycle of heavier daytime caffeine.
