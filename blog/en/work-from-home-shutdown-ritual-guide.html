Why Missing a Commute Quietly Fuels Remote Burnout

A physical commute used to signal "work is over." Without a clear boundary cue, Slack thoughts bleed into evenings and rob you of deep sleep recovery.

The 3-Minute Parking Lot (Closing the Zeigarnik Loop)

Write every open loop onto tomorrow’s list with its very first next step. Once captured externally, your working memory stops sending urgent reminders at 9 PM.

Physical Transition Cue to Replace the Commute

Change clothes, wash your face, step outside for a 5-minute walk, or hide the laptop out of sight so your living room feels like home again.

FAQ

Why do I keep thinking about work emails at night when working from home?

Psychologists call this the Zeigarnik Effect: uncaptured unfinished tasks stay active in memory until you write down a concrete plan for tomorrow.

How long should a remote shutdown ritual take?

Just 3 to 5 minutes at the end of your workday—short enough to repeat daily without friction.
