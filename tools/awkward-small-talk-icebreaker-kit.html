    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the easiest alternative to asking "What do you do for work?"

    Try "What’s been keeping you busy or excited lately?" or "How do you know [host/event]?"—it lets people share hobbies, travel, or work whichever they enjoy most.

    How do I leave a conversation politely without feeling rude?

    Give a warm bridge ("I’ve really enjoyed talking with you!"), a simple reason ("I’m going to grab some water / say hi to my friend"), and a smile.
