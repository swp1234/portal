    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is it called "revenge" bedtime procrastination?

    The term became popular to describe sacrificing sleep for late-night leisure as a subconscious pushback against daytime schedules that leave little or no personal freedom—even when you know you will be exhausted tomorrow.

    Why does strict willpower fail against bedtime procrastination?

    Self-regulation capacity is lowest late at night after a full day of demands (Kroese et al., 2014). Fixing daytime autonomy and pre-staging bedtime hygiene earlier in the evening works far better than forcing willpower at 1 AM.
