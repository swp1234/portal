    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why am I completely exhausted at 5 PM during my first month at a new job?

    Processing unfamiliar names, acronyms, social norms, and workflows simultaneously places heavy load on working memory—fatigue drops sharply once routines become automatic around weeks 6–10.

    Will asking lots of questions in my first month make me look unqualified?

    The opposite—batching thoughtful, context-rich questions during your first 60 days is expected and signals ownership far more than guessing silently.
