    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I feel more tired on Monday after sleeping 11 hours on Sunday?

    Waking 3–4 hours later than usual shifts your circadian clock backward (social jetlag), delaying Sunday night melatonin and leaving Monday morning feeling like flying across three time zones.

    What is the healthiest way to catch up on weekday sleep debt?

    Go to bed 45–60 minutes earlier on Friday/Saturday, wake within 60–90 minutes of your weekday time, and add a brief 20–25 minute early-afternoon nap if needed.
