    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do I feel dizzy or lightheaded on highways and bridges?

    Gripping the wheel tightly and breathing shallowly (mild hyperventilation) lowers CO2 slightly and triggers brief lightheadedness—long slow exhales and cool AC air settle it quickly. Always pull over safely if you feel faint.

    How do I rebuild highway confidence after avoiding it for months?

    Use graded exposure: start with a single 1-exit hop on a quiet weekend morning in the right lane, repeat until comfortable, then gradually add distance.
