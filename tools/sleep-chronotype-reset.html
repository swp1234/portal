    Why do sleep calculators use 90-minute cycles?

    Human sleep progresses through light sleep, slow-wave deep sleep, and REM stages in ultradian cycles averaging roughly 90 minutes. Waking near the end of a full cycle reduces grogginess (sleep inertia).

    How many hours before bed should caffeine stop?

    Because caffeine has an average half-life of 5 to 7 hours, stopping caffeine 8 to 10 hours before bedtime helps preserve deep slow-wave sleep architecture.
