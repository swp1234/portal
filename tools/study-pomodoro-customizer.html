    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is 25/5 or 50/10 better for studying?

    Neither is universally best: 15/3 or 25/5 shines when starting resistance is high or fatigue is heavy, while 45/10 or 50/10 works better for deep coding, essays, or complex math once flow begins.

    Why should study breaks be smartphone-free?

    Brief quiet offline breaks allow the brain’s replay mechanisms to consolidate new memories, whereas scrolling social media crowds out freshly learned material.
