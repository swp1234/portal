FAQ

Does losing my patience occasionally permanently harm my child?

Attachment research consistently finds that warm, honest repair after a rupture builds strong emotional security and models healthy accountability.

What does "good enough parenting" actually mean?

Coined by pediatrician Donald Winnicott, it means meeting core emotional needs reliably without chasing exhausting, unrealistic perfection.
