    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does Googling symptoms make anxiety worse instead of better?

    Known as cyberchondria, online symptom searching exposes you to rare worst-case conditions and reinforces an intolerance-of-uncertainty cycle where temporary reassurance wears off rapidly.

    When should I actually contact a doctor or urgent care?

    This interactive page is for reflection on worry loops only—never medical advice. Always seek prompt professional medical evaluation for severe, sudden, worsening, or persistent physical symptoms.
