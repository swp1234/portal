    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do you tell career burnout apart from being in the wrong career?

    When exhaustion persists even after real rest and stems from mismatch with core daily tasks and values—not just workload volume—a role or career pivot deserves structured evaluation.

    Why do small 14-day experiments beat endless pro/con lists?

    Design-thinking career research (Burnett & Evans) shows real conversations and mini-prototypes reveal day-to-day reality far more accurately than abstract mental speculation.
