    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does checking just one work email on Saturday ruin my relaxation?

    Even if you do not reply, reading an unresolved problem activates the Zeigarnik effect—keeping your brain passively rehearsing fixes instead of recovering.

    Why should I use Schedule-Send for Monday 9 AM instead of replying Sunday?

    Sending emails on Sunday pressures teammates to work on weekends and immediately pulls you into live back-and-forth threads.
