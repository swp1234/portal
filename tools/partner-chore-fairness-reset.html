    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the invisible mental load in a household?

    It is the behind-the-scenes cognitive work of noticing what is running low, remembering deadlines, planning schedules, and delegating—even when someone else does the physical chore.

    Why does "just tell me what to do" still feel exhausting?

    Because acting as the sole project manager forces one partner to hold every family detail in working memory continuously. Full domain ownership solves the root cause.
