    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does stopping hyperfocus feel physically uncomfortable?

    Task switching requires disengaging dopamine-rich engagement and reloading executive control, creating brief friction—especially for ADHD brains.

    How does a 1-line breadcrumb note help me stop?

    Offloading your exact next micro-step onto paper reassures working memory that the thread will not vanish, cutting resistance immediately.
