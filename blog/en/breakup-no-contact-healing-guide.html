Why Checking Their Socials Resets Your Healing Clock

Neuroimaging studies show romantic rejection lights up craving and pain circuits simultaneously. One quick profile peek delivers an unpredictable dopamine jolt that prolongs withdrawal.

The 10-Minute Urge-Surfing Delay Rule

Promise yourself you will wait 10 full minutes before sending anything. Write the raw message in a private local note, take 6 slow exhales, and re-read how you will feel tomorrow morning.

Replacing the Evening Text Habit Before It Strikes

Urges spike at predictable times—commutes, Sunday nights, and late evenings. Pre-assigning a friend call, walk, or audio book fills the exact slot where texts used to live.

FAQ

How long does the hardest part of breakup no-contact last?

Most people notice acute hourly urges soften noticeably after 2 to 4 weeks of consistent no-contact and muted social feeds.

Does writing an unsent letter actually help?

Yes. Expressing the emotion privately discharges the urgency without reopening a painful back-and-forth cycle.
