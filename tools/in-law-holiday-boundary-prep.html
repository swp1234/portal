    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I deflect intrusive family questions without starting an argument?

    Use the 3-beat formula: brief warm acknowledgment ("Thanks for asking!"), non-defensive boundary ("We’re keeping that simple for now"), and an immediate curious question about them.

    Why does announcing your exit time upfront help so much?

    Setting expectations warmly upon arrival prevents sudden guilt trips when you leave and lets you enjoy the hours you are there without feeling trapped.
