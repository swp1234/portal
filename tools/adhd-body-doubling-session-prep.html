    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does body doubling help ADHD and procrastinating brains?

    A calm, non-judgmental partner acts as an external executive-function anchor—providing gentle social presence, a clear start/stop boundary, and lower emotional boredom without micromanagement.

    Does virtual or video-off body doubling work too?

    Yes. Even a 2-line chat check-in ("Starting at :00 on invoices, back at :25!") or a quiet coworking room gives your brain the external start cue it needs.
