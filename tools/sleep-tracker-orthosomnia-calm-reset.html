    Frequently Asked Questions

    How accurate are smartwatches at measuring Deep and REM sleep?

    Consumer wrist trackers estimate total sleep time reasonably well, but clinical validation studies show they often misclassify individual Deep/REM stages compared to lab EEG.

    Can seeing a bad sleep score actually make me feel more tired?

    Yes—controlled studies on "placebo sleep" found people told they had poor sleep scored worse on daytime alertness tasks regardless of how well they actually slept.
