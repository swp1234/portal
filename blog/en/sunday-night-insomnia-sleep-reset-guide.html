FAQ

Why can I fall asleep easily Friday and Saturday but not Sunday?

Late weekend wake-ups delay your melatonin curve (social jetlag), while Monday anticipation adds performance pressure to fall asleep quickly.

What should I do tonight if I am still awake after 20 minutes?

Skip clock-checking, step into dim light to read a light paper page or run a gentle cognitive shuffle until eyelids grow heavy, then return to bed.
