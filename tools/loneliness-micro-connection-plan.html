    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do tiny "micro-connections" meaningfully reduce loneliness?

    Social psychology research (Sandstrom & Dunn; Liu et al., 2022) shows brief warm interactions—like a 2-sentence check-in text or friendly chat with a barista—significantly increase feelings of belonging without requiring high social energy.

    Why does scrolling social media often make loneliness worse?

    Passive feed consumption triggers upward social comparison while offering zero reciprocal human responsiveness, leaving the brain’s social-connection circuit hungrier than before.
