    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between healthy guilt and false (neurotic) guilt?

    Healthy guilt arises when our actions genuinely violate our own values and motivates clean repair. False guilt arises when we honor normal limits (resting, saying no, not rescuing someone) and drains energy without helping anyone.

    Why does setting a healthy boundary often trigger a spike of guilt at first?

    If you were praised for being low-maintenance or endlessly accommodating, your nervous system tags self-protection as "selfish" until repeated safe repetitions prove boundaries actually strengthen honest relationships.
