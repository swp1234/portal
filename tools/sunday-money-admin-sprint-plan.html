    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do tiny 5-minute errands cause so much background stress?

    Each unclosed administrative errand occupies working memory as an open Zeigarnik loop—creating disproportionate mental fatigue until you capture it in one list and batch-close it.

    Why limit a life-admin sprint to 20 minutes?

    A short, bounded 20-minute window eliminates emotional overwhelm and lowers start friction, yet is long enough to knock out 3–5 nagging errands.
