FAQ

What is the clearest sign a workplace culture will not improve?

When documented commitments on workload, respect, or resourcing are repeatedly broken and honest feedback is met with defensiveness rather than repair.

How do I protect my mental health while preparing to leave?

Shift from emotional over-investment to calm professionalism: put priorities in writing, log off at clear hours, and direct saved energy into your next chapter.
