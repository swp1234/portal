FAQ

Does a 48-hour dopamine detox really reset attention?

Stepping away from rapid-fire algorithmic feeds for 48 hours lowers cue reactivity noticeably, making reading, deep work, and quiet rest feel much more engaging on Monday.

Can I listen to music or meet friends during a realistic dopamine detox?

Yes! Healthy analog pleasures like walks, live conversation, cooking, and full-album music support recovery—only infinite algorithmic scrolling is paused.
