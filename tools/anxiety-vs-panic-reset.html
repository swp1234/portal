    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the main difference between an anxiety surge and a panic attack?

    Anticipatory anxiety usually builds gradually around a specific stressor or worry loop and can linger at moderate intensity. A panic attack spikes abruptly within minutes with intense physical symptoms like racing pulse, shortness of breath, or dizziness, then peaks and naturally subsides.

    Why is fighting a panic surge often counterproductive?

    Telling yourself 'I must stop this immediately' signals additional threat to the amygdala, releasing a second wave of adrenaline. Dropping resistance, extending exhalations, and riding the wave lets the autonomic spike peak and pass faster.
