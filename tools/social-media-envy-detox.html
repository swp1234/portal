    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does passive social media scrolling trigger envy so reliably?

    Algorithms surface peak achievements from hundreds of peers simultaneously, tricking our evolutionary status radar into feeling behind even when our real life is going well.

    Can envy ever be useful?

    Psychologists distinguish malicious envy from benign envy—using a brief pang of envy simply as information about a skill, freedom, or habit you want to cultivate for yourself.
