    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do introverts feel physically exhausted after enjoyable social events?

    Socializing involves rapid processing of voices, facial expressions, noise filtering, and self-monitoring—activating high arousal that requires quiet low-stimulus recovery afterward (Little, 2014).

    How long does it take to recover from a social hangover?

    Light social fatigue often clears after 1–2 quiet hours; multi-day conferences or holiday weekends may take one full low-obligation recovery day.
