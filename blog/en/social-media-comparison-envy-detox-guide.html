Why Passive Scrolling Triggers Upward Comparison

Research consistently finds passive feed consumption—watching others’ curated vacations, promotions, and physiques without interacting—drives sharper mood drops than active messaging.

Turning Toxic Envy Into Useful Information

Envy is rarely about wanting another person’s whole life; it is usually an arrow pointing toward one unmet desire of your own (rest, creative freedom, recognition, or community).

FAQ

Is muting friends on social media rude?

Not at all—muting is completely invisible and often preserves real-life friendships by preventing quiet resentment from curated posts.

Do I need to quit social media forever to stop comparing?

No; shifting from passive endless feeds to intentional messaging and curating your follow list removes most comparison spikes.
