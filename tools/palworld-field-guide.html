    ONE RULE FOR EVERY PATCH

    Build systems, not one perfect answer

    1

    Name the bottleneck

    Is progress blocked by materials, travel, capture chance, survivability, work speed or information?

    2

    Change one layer

    Swap one Pal role, one production chain or one piece of equipment before rebuilding everything.

    3

    Measure one loop

    Run one capture trip, production cycle or boss attempt and record what actually failed.

    4

    Keep the reusable gain

    Prioritize storage, routes, breeding records and role coverage that remain valuable after a patch.
