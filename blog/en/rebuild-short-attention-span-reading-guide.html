FAQ

How long does it take to rebuild reading focus after heavy phone use?

Most adults notice deeper immersion within 10–14 days of reading paper books for just 10–15 phone-free minutes daily.

Do audiobooks count when rebuilding attention?

Audiobooks are wonderful for stories, while pairing them with 10 minutes of physical paper reading specifically retrains visual deep-reading circuits.
