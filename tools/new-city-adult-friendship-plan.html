    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does it take to make a real friend in a new city?

    University of Kansas researcher Jeffrey Hall found it takes roughly 50 hours of shared time to move from acquaintance to casual friend, and 90+ hours for a close friend—which is why weekly recurring groups accelerate it so much.

    What is the "Liking Gap" in social psychology?

    Boothby et al. (2018) found that after meeting someone new, people systematically underestimate how much the other person liked them and enjoyed the conversation.
