FAQ

Does grayscale really help reduce phone addiction?

Yes—controlled studies (Holte & Ferraro, 2020) found grayscale screens reduced daily phone usage by 30–40 minutes on average by dimming visual reward triggers.

Do I have to delete social media completely?

No! Simply removing apps from your home screen (requiring deliberate search or browser login) preserves usefulness while cutting mindless pickups.
