    Frequently Asked Questions & Cognitive Science

    What is cognitive load and mental RAM saturation?

    Cognitive load refers to the total volume of mental effort actively processed in working memory. Similar to computer RAM, working memory has a strictly limited capacity (typically 4 to 7 discrete chunks). When overwhelmed by open tasks, digital notifications, and context switches, processing speed drops dramatically, producing subjective brain fog.

    How does context-switching trigger prefrontal fatigue?

    Every shift between tasks incurs an 'attention residue' penalty. The prefrontal cortex must reconfigure executive control rules and suppress previous schemas, rapidly burning glucose and neurotransmitters like dopamine and norepinephrine.

    What is the difference between working memory overload and clinical brain fog?

    Working memory overload is an acute, reversible state caused by environmental overstimulation or multi-tasking. Clinical brain fog may stem from chronic inflammation, sleep apnea, metabolic imbalance, or long-COVID. If cognitive slowing persists despite lifestyle restructuring, medical consultation is indicated.

    How can I quickly decompress cognitive overload?

    Perform an externalized 'brain dump' onto physical paper, shut down non-essential digital browser tabs, take a 10-minute walk without audio, and use structured 25-minute single-tasking intervals.
