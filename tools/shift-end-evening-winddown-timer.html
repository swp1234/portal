    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does a warm shower before bed help me fall asleep faster?

    Warm water draws blood toward the skin; when you step into a cool room, core body temperature drops rapidly—one of the strongest biological signals for sleep onset.

    How long should an evening wind-down buffer be?

    30 to 45 minutes is ideal for most adults—long enough for cortisol and bright-light suppression to fade without feeling rigid.
