    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is it okay to declare "inbox bankruptcy" on old unread promos?

    Yes! Archiving (not deleting) old non-urgent messages clears visual overwhelm immediately while keeping everything searchable if you ever need it.

    How should I reply to an email I avoided for two weeks?

    Skip long self-blaming apologies: write "Thanks for your patience!" and jump straight into a clear, helpful 2-sentence answer.
