    Frequently Asked Questions

    What are avoidant deactivation strategies in attachment theory?

    Deactivation strategies are automatic protective habits—such as focusing on a partner’s minor flaws, suddenly feeling numb, or burying yourself in work—that the nervous system uses to restore a sense of independence when intimacy feels overwhelming.

    Why does adding a specific return time transform asking for space?

    Saying only "I need space" leaves the other person bracing for abandonment, which often triggers anxious chasing. Adding "I will check in tomorrow at 7 PM" calms both nervous systems simultaneously.
