    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does burnout often return within weeks of going back to work?

    Maslach and Leiter’s research shows burnout is driven by chronic workload-control mismatch; returning to the exact same unpaced boundaries rapidly recreates exhaustion.

    What is a healthy pacing rule during burnout re-entry?

    Aiming for ~75–80% planned capacity with explicit trade-off conversations ("if we add X, what moves?") keeps output reliable while leaving room for real recovery.
