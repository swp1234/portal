    Why does emotional stress show up as jaw clenching or tight shoulders?

    When the autonomic nervous system detects chronic urgency or vigilance, motor nerves preemptively brace protective muscle groups—especially the masseter (jaw), upper trapezius (shoulders), and diaphragm—even when there is no physical danger.

    How does Jacobson Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR) release tension faster than trying to 'just relax'?

    Gently engaging a tense muscle for 5 seconds followed by a 10-second slow exhale creates a clear neuromuscular contrast signal (post-isometric relaxation), helping the brain locate and drop unconscious bracing.
