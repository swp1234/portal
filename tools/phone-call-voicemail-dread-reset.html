    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do so many capable adults feel anxious making simple phone calls?

    Real-time voice calls remove editing time and facial cues, triggering anticipatory social anxiety—even when the call itself takes under 2 minutes.

    What helps most if my mind goes blank mid-call?

    Keep a 3-bullet sticky note in front of you and use a friendly buffer phrase like "Give me just one second to pull that up on my screen"—nobody minds a 3-second pause.
