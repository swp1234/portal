    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the Cognitive Shuffle technique for falling asleep faster?

    Developed by cognitive scientist Dr. Luc Beaudoin, the Cognitive Shuffle involves picturing harmless, unrelated objects (e.g., following letters of a neutral word) to disrupt analytical worry and trigger the brain’s natural sleep-onset state.

    Why do cringe memories and worries feel 10x louder at midnight?

    When external distractions disappear and the prefrontal cortex fatigues at night, the brain’s default-mode network amplifies unresolved worries—making simple written parking and gentle visual redirection far more effective than debating thoughts.
