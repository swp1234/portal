    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between feeling tired and entering burnout?

    Normal tiredness usually lifts after a good weekend or sleep; burnout involves persistent exhaustion, growing cynicism/detachment, and feeling depleted even after rest.

    Why does boundary setting matter more than productivity hacks for burnout?

    Maslach & Leiter’s burnout research highlights chronic workload-control mismatch as a primary driver; restoring sustainable limits addresses the root load rather than squeezing more effort.
