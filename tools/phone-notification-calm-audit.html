    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do red notification badges cause subtle anxiety?

    Red visual dots exploit human salience and incomplete-task tension (the Zeigarnik effect), creating a nagging sense of unfinished obligations even when the alert is just a promotional coupon.

    Does keeping my phone face-down on my desk actually help?

    Face-down helps slightly, but Ward et al. (2017) found that moving the phone out of sight (in a bag or another room) yields the largest boost in working memory and fluid attention.
