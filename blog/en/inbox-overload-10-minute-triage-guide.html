FAQ

Will I lose important emails if I bulk-archive old unread promos?

Archiving removes visual clutter from your inbox while keeping every message 100% searchable whenever you need it.

How often should I check email for lower stress?

Most knowledge workers feel noticeably calmer checking in 2–3 dedicated blocks (e.g., 11 AM and 4 PM) with desktop popups turned off.
