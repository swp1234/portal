    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does outdoor light on a cloudy winter day really help?

    Yes. Even heavily overcast outdoor daylight provides roughly 2,000 to 10,000 lux—dramatically stronger than typical indoor rooms (150–400 lux) for signaling circadian alertness.

    Is this a diagnosis for Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD)?

    No. This is an educational lifestyle and light-routine planner running privately in your browser. If seasonal mood changes seriously impair daily functioning, please speak with a healthcare professional.
