Why Holding Open Tasks in Memory Causes Sunday Anxiety

Masicampo & Baumeister (2011) showed uncaptured open goals keep pinging working memory—yet simply writing a concrete plan silences that intrusive mental buzz immediately.

The Rule of 3 Weekly Wins (Not a 25-Item Wish List)

Long lists paralyze execution. Picking 3 clear weekly wins ensures that even during a noisy week, what truly matters finishes smoothly.

Schedule Two Recovery Blocks Before Meetings Fill the Calendar

Treat exercise, quiet reading, or a free evening like real appointments so burnout never creeps in unnoticed.

FAQ

When is the best time to do a 10-minute weekly review?

Friday afternoon (before logging off) or Sunday afternoon works best—giving your brain total closure before Monday morning.

Why limit weekly priorities to just 3 main wins?

Focusing on 3 high-impact outcomes cuts decision fatigue and raises real completion rates far above 20-item to-do lists.
