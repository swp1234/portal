    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do people with ADHD or busy schedules misjudge how long getting ready takes?

    Time blindness and the Planning Fallacy cause the brain to count only the core drive while omitting invisible transition tasks like finding keys, locking up, parking, and walking inside.

    What is the easiest single change to stop running 10 minutes late?

    Calculate backwards from arriving 10 minutes early, add +15 minutes for transitions, and set a hard "stop all new tasks" alarm 20 minutes before leaving.
