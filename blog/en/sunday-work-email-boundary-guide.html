Your nervous system needs genuine off-duty hours to clear cortisol. Answering one non-urgent thread on Sunday night trains colleagues to expect 7-day availability.

Why "Just Checking" Costs 3 Hours of Mental Rest

Reading a thorny message you cannot resolve until Monday triggers an open cognitive loop (Zeigarnik effect) that keeps adrenaline elevated through bedtime.

The Delay-Send Monday 9 AM Habit

If inspiration genuinely strikes on Sunday, draft the reply offline and schedule it for Monday 9:05 AM so you protect team norms and your own reputation for calm focus.

Set a Clear Escalation Rule Once

Tell your team: "I unplug from email on weekends to stay sharp Monday–Friday; for true emergencies, please text or call." Real emergencies almost never happen.

FAQ

Does checking work email on Sunday reduce Monday stress?

Usually the opposite—previewing unresolved work problems triggers Sunday night insomnia without actually completing the task.

How do I stop replying on weekends without looking uncommitted?

Communicate your weekday responsiveness clearly, provide an emergency phone path, and schedule any drafted messages for Monday morning.
