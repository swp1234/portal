    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do small moments trigger disproportionately huge emotional reactions?

    Emotional triggers occur when a present-day cue (tone, silence, criticism) resembles an earlier unresolved threat. The amygdala fires a rapid survival alarm before the logical prefrontal cortex finishes checking current facts.

    How long does the physiological surge of an emotional trigger last?

    Neuroanatomist Jill Bolte Taylor notes that the initial chemical surge of adrenaline and cortisol peaks and clears in roughly 90 seconds unless we keep re-fueling it with repetitive mental stories.
