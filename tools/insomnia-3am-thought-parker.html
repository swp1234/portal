    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do so many people wake up around 3 AM?

    Between 2 AM and 4 AM, deep slow-wave sleep tapers off and REM/light sleep cycles dominate while core body temperature and cortisol begin their gradual pre-dawn rise—making stress thoughts much louder if you wake briefly.

    What is cognitive shuffling for insomnia?

    Developed by cognitive scientist Luc Beaudoin, cognitive shuffling involves visualizing brief, emotionally neutral, unrelated words or scenes so the analytical worry network powers down and lets sleep onset resume.
