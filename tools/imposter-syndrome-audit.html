    What are the 5 imposter syndrome archetypes?

    Dr. Valerie Young categorizes imposter feelings into five competence rules: The Perfectionist, The Expert, The Natural Genius, The Soloist, and The Superperson. Identifying which rule drives self-doubt makes reframing concrete.

    How does an Evidence Proof Vault counter imposter feelings?

    Imposter cycles discount wins as luck. Recording observable skills, solved problems, and positive feedback creates factual counter-evidence when doubt resurfaces.
