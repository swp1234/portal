Falling Asleep Is Not the Same as Deep Restorative Sleep

Controlled sleep-lab studies show late-afternoon caffeine trims slow-wave deep sleep even in people who fall asleep within 15 minutes.

The 9-to-10-Hour Rule Backed by Meta-Analysis

A 2023 Sleep Medicine Reviews meta-analysis concluded standard coffee (~100mg) should stop at least 8.8 hours before bedtime to prevent measurable sleep loss.

FAQ

What is the ideal last time to drink coffee if I sleep at 11 PM?

For an 11 PM bedtime, finishing your last caffeinated drink around 1:00–2:00 PM (9–10 hours prior) protects slow-wave deep sleep best.

Does decaf coffee still contain caffeine?

Decaf typically contains only 2–7mg of caffeine versus 95–150mg in regular coffee—small enough for most people to enjoy safely in the early afternoon.
