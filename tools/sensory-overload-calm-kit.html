    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does sensory overload feel physically painful or make people suddenly irritable?

    Roughly 15–20% of people carry high Sensory Processing Sensitivity (SPS). Their brains process subtleties, noise, light, and social cues more deeply, meaning crowded inputs fill working memory and trigger fight-or-flight faster.

    Why doesn’t scrolling on a phone relieve sensory overload?

    Even while sitting still, rapid video cuts, bright blue light, and emotional headlines continue flooding visual and cognitive channels. True sensory decompression needs 5 minutes of genuine low input.
