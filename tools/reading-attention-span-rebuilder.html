    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can a short attention span caused by social media really be rebuilt?

    Yes. Because reading circuits rely on neuroplasticity, even 10–15 minutes of daily uninterrupted paper reading noticeably rebuilds deep-reading stamina within a few weeks.

    Why does paper or an e-ink reader beat reading on a smartphone?

    Phones carry built-in notification and tab-switching triggers that fragment comprehension, whereas single-purpose books or e-ink readers preserve spatial page memory.
