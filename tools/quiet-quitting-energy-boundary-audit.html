    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is setting strict work hours the same as not caring about my job?

    No—working rested and focused during agreed hours consistently outperforms chronically exhausted overtime in accuracy, creativity, and retention.

    How do I push back on extra work without sounding uncooperative?

    Frame it around shared quality and priority trade-offs: "Happy to prioritize X—would you like me to pause Y until next Tuesday?"
