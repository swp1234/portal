    Frequently Asked Questions

    What are the 4 steps of Nonviolent Communication (OFNR)?

    Developed by Dr. Marshall Rosenberg, OFNR stands for Observation (neutral fact without judgment), Feeling (your internal emotion), Need (the universal value behind the feeling), and Request (a concrete, doable action).

    What if the other person still responds defensively?

    NVC lowers unnecessary friction, though you cannot control another person’s reaction. If boundaries are repeatedly dismissed or reversed, shift to concise broken-record limits.
