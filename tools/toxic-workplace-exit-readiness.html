    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I know if my workplace is toxic versus just demanding?

    Demanding jobs still offer clarity, mutual respect, fair credit, and recovery windows. Toxic environments combine chronic disrespect, shifting blame, broken commitments, and sustained fear of speaking honestly.

    Should I quit immediately without another job lined up?

    Unless your immediate physical or mental safety requires an urgent break, building a calm 60–90 day financial and resume runway while lowering emotional over-investment usually gives you far better negotiating leverage.
