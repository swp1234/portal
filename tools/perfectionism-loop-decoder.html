    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why does perfectionism often look like procrastination or self-sabotage?

    Clinical perfectionism ties self-worth to flawless outcomes. When the brain predicts criticism or imperfection, delaying or quitting early temporarily shields your ego ('I did not fail—I just did not really try'), creating a self-sabotage loop.

    How does the 5-minute 'Ugly First Draft' rule break perfectionist paralysis?

    Intentionally lowering the quality bar for just 5 minutes disarms threat-based avoidance in the amygdala. Once a messy draft exists, editing feels dramatically safer than starting from a blank page.
